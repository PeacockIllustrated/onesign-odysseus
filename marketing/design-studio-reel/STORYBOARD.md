# Design Your Sign — 4-Step Studio Reel — Storyboard

> A free, no-login web studio where UK business owners design their own folded-aluminium sign in live 3D, light it up day-to-night, and send it for a quote — told as a tight four-step product film that ends on a real success screen.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) · **Length:** 38s · **Frame rate:** 30fps · **British English · sound-off first**


The reel is a faithful reconstruction of the real `/design` studio (dark 3D stage + frosted white wizard dock, the four steps Size → Artwork → Light → Send, the day/night hero beat, glow colours, and the real success screen). Built programmatically in Remotion so every frame is on-brand and reproducible.
