Mascot concept

The 1nesign crew

Every member of staff is the Onesign mark. The O is the body and the 1 inside it is lit in that person's colour, the way a halo-lit letter glows on a fascia. The mark itself is never redrawn: it's the real logo, with eyes, stick limbs and the kit each role carries.

The line-up

Five roles, one mark

Each follows a job through the building, in the order a sign gets made.

Designer

Pencil, swatch fan. Taps out the idea.

Fabricator

Goggles, welding glove. Throws sparks.

Driver

Flat cap, van keys. Always on the move.

Fitter

Hard hat, drill. Puts the sign up.

Project manager

Headset, clipboard. Ticks it all off.

How one is made

The icon from the logo, untouched: an O with the 1 cut out of it.

Light the 1

Eye placement

Where the eyes go

The same designer with eight eye positions. Every one leaves the mark untouched. Only where the eyes sit changes, and with it the character.

Rules

Keeping it on brand

  • Never redraw the markThe body is the logo icon exactly. Props may overlap it; nothing reshapes it.
  • Colour lives in the 1The body stays ink. A role's colour appears only in the lit 1, the hands and the kit.
  • One prop per roleA second prop is allowed only if it animates. Anything more reads as clutter at sticker size.
  • Eyes either side of the stemThey sit off-centre on purpose. Centring them fights the mark.
