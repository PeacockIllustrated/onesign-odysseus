# Brochure Writeups — Onesign & Digital Portfolio

Reference pack for the Onesign & Digital brochure. Each project has a detailed writeup (app summary + brand identity + design suggestion) plus a one-paragraph "angle" below — the single aspect to lead with when presenting the product to a prospective customer.

---

## Product index

### [Persimmon Fulfillment — Signage Portal](persimmon-fulfillment.md)
**Angle:** *Bespoke B2B ordering portals for your supply chain.* Persimmon's site managers order construction signage from a curated, Persimmon-only catalogue — with basket, checkout, custom-size configurator, and admin-side PO/delivery-note uploads. Pitch as "we replace your customer's procurement spreadsheet with their own branded shop."

### [OneSign · Lynx (onesign-qr)](onesign-lynx.md)
**Angle:** *Our flagship productised SaaS — the one you can buy today.* Lead with the **managed QR code** story: print once, redirect anywhere, update from the dashboard without reprinting. Then stack the adjacent modules (bio pages, NFC merch, review funnels, team accounts) to show it's a complete small-business presence stack. Pair with the physical merch fulfilled by Onesign & Digital — software + signage is the differentiator.

### [Onesign Odysseus](onesign-odysseus.md)
**Angle:** *We eat our own dog food.* This is the platform **we** run the business on — quotes, artwork sign-off, production Kanban, shop-floor tablets, purchase orders, invoices, deliveries, CRM — end-to-end, replacing a £55/user/month SaaS. "If we can build the system that runs a signage agency, we can build the system that runs your operation." The tokenised public links (artwork sign-off, proof-of-delivery) are a concrete demo.

### [Mapleleaf Roots](mapleleaf-roots.md)
**Angle:** *Deep enterprise systems for franchise networks.* Four-tier role hierarchy (HQ → Area → Site → Employee), a **2D drag-drop store-fitting configurator** that auto-generates an Onesign quote, planogram management down to the individual shelf slot, and network-wide promo campaign rollouts with auto-generated print packs. This is the "we build the software **and** the physical signage the software orders" story at scale — one integrated pipeline from HQ campaign → local install.

### [Durham Stickmakers](durham-stickmakers.md)
**Angle:** *Close to a standard site we deliver — and even this is fully owner-managed.* Public face is a heritage charity site with shop, blog, workshop bookings, and Gift-Aid donations. The story is the admin side: the charity owner (non-technical) adds new bespoke sticks with drag-drop photos, sets a price, hits Publish, and it appears in the shop with a Stripe product live-created in the background. Same goes for blog posts, workshops, and static page copy. Pitch as "your standard small-business site, but you never call us for a content change again."
