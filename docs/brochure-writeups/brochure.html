01 · CASE STUDY
Persimmon
Fulfillment.

The branded ordering portal that replaces a site manager's procurement spreadsheet.

Overview

A closed, single-tenant ordering portal built for Persimmon Homes. Site managers on active developments browse a Persimmon-only catalogue of approved construction signage, configure custom sizes, add to basket, and check out — all under Persimmon branding.

On the admin side, Onesign staff manage the product library, upload POs and delivery notes, and track fulfillment against real orders in one place.

Key features

  • Category-based product browsing with live search
  • Custom-size configurator for bespoke signs
  • Full basket, checkout & order confirmation flow
  • Admin tooling: PO upload, delivery-note upload, order management
  • Branded splash + polished motion design throughout
What this demonstrates

Replacing a customer's procurement spreadsheet with their own branded shop. If you supply a national client with the same products every month, we'll turn that relationship into a portal.

02 · PRODUCT
OneSign · Lynx.

Our productised SaaS — the one you can buy today. Print a QR once; redirect it forever.

Overview

Lynx is a complete business-presence platform for UK small businesses. Bio pages, managed QR codes, NFC merchandise, and review funnels — one dashboard, sub-200ms redirects, no lock-in.

The killer feature: a printed Lynx QR code always encodes a Onesign-owned short link. Destinations update from the dashboard with no reprinting. Analytics are captured at the redirect; PII is not stored.

Modules

  • Bio Pages — block-based editor with templates & forms
  • Managed QR — print once, redirect anywhere, indefinitely
  • Review Funnels — 5★-to-Google, low-rating-to-private
  • Team Accounts — multi-user orgs with role hierarchy
  • Shopfront — branded NFC cards, A-frames, review boards
Software + signage

The physical merch is fulfilled by our own production floor — the same place that ships Mapleleaf forecourt totems. That's the Onesign unfair advantage: your customers can order both in one place.

Odysseus.

We eat our own dog food. This is the platform we run Onesign & Digital on.

Overview

Odysseus is our internal production-management platform. It replaces Clarity Go — a third-party SaaS at ~£55 per user per month — with a bespoke, Onesign-owned system that runs the entire pipeline from customer quote to invoice.

Every job we make for every client passes through this system. Staff use the admin; clients interact via tokenised links for artwork sign-off and proof-of-delivery.

MapleleafROOTS

One integrated pipeline from HQ campaign to local install, across the entire franchise network.

Overview

A bespoke operations platform for Mapleleaf Petroleum Group's UK forecourt-franchise network. Roots handles the full lifecycle of a franchise site — from initial fit-out through day-to-day operations to network-wide promotional rollouts.

Four-tier hierarchy

HQ AdminEntire network · templates, campaigns, catalogue
Area ManagerGeographic region · site onboarding, compliance
Site ManagerOne site · planogram, campaign confirm, staff
EmployeeTask level · substitutions, installs, restocks
  • 2D drag-drop floor-plan configurator auto-generating a Onesign quote

05 · SMALL BUSINESS
Durham Stickmakers.

A heritage charity site — and the CMS behind it. Your standard site, fully owner-managed.

Overview

A full website rebuild for Durham Stick Makers, a County Durham charity preserving the endangered craft of stickmaking. Public face: a warm, editorial site with shop, blog, workshop bookings and Gift-Aid donations. One of a kind

What makes it different

The admin side. The charity owner — non-technical — adds a new bespoke stick by uploading photos (drag and drop), writing a description, setting a price, and hitting Publish. A Stripe product is created live in the background. It's on sale in 60 seconds.

  • Drag-drop image uploader (Supabase Storage, up to 6 photos, reorderable)
  • Stripe Checkout — unique sticks flip to "Sold" automatically
  • Workshop calendar with capacity & online bookings
  • Blog CMS + CMS-lite for static pages & site config
  • Full SEO kit: JSON-LD, sitemap, OpenGraph on every page
The pitch

Your standard small-business site — but you never call us for a content change again. Listings, prices, photos, blog posts, workshops, static copy: all yours.
